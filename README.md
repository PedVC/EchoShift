# Echo Shift — Fragments of Time 

A 3D platformer and puzzle game made with **Godot 4.5.1** (GDScript), for Windows.
You travel through worlds of floating islands, each made up of several stretches
and a portal that leads to the next, switching between six different challenges.

## The game

Each world has between 3 and 7 stretches, and the difficulty increases gradually.
The stretches alternate between six activities, never repeating the same one
twice in a row, not even when moving from one world to the next:

- **Parkour** — small platforms, zigzags and different heights, using jump and dash.
- **Storm escape** — a red front advances behind you. Run, jump over obstacles and don't fall behind.
- **Echo puzzle** — record your movement as an echo and anchor it on blue bases to materialise the bridge.
- **Path choice** — a wide, safe route, or a narrow one with lasers and bonuses.
- **Moving platforms** — platforms that travel and oscillate, carrying you along.
- **Sentinel arena** — drones chase you across the island. Neutralise them with the dash to open the exit.

## Features

- Continuous travel from world to world, with progressive difficulty
- Mastery bonuses for each stretch, rewarding performance and choices
- Checkpoints, a credit wallet and a shop for skins and consumables
- Game modes: Continuous Journey, Pure Challenge (no aids) and Daily Journey (route varies by date)
- No real-money purchases and no loot boxes
- Models, scenery, effects and sounds generated by code

## Technology

- Godot Engine 4.5.1
- GDScript
- GL Compatibility renderer
