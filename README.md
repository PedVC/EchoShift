## 🇬🇧 English

A 3D platformer and puzzle game made with **Godot 4.5.1** (GDScript), for Windows.
You travel through worlds of floating islands, each made up of several stretches
and a portal that leads to the next, switching between six different challenges.

> **Free to play and share.** You are welcome to download, play and share the
> game. Modifying the game or this repository is not allowed. See
> [Copyright and permissions](#copyright-and-permissions).

### The game

Each world has between 3 and 7 stretches, and the difficulty increases gradually.
The stretches alternate between six activities, never repeating the same one
twice in a row, not even when moving from one world to the next:

- **Parkour** — small platforms, zigzags and different heights, using jump and dash.
- **Storm escape** — a red front advances behind you. Run, jump over obstacles and don't fall behind.
- **Echo puzzle** — record your movement as an echo and anchor it on blue bases to materialise the bridge.
- **Path choice** — a wide, safe route, or a narrow one with lasers and bonuses.
- **Moving platforms** — platforms that travel and oscillate, carrying you along.
- **Sentinel arena** — drones chase you across the island. Neutralise them with the dash to open the exit.

### Features

- Continuous travel from world to world, with progressive difficulty
- Mastery bonuses for each stretch, rewarding performance and choices
- Checkpoints, a credit wallet and a shop for skins and consumables
- Game modes: Continuous Journey, Pure Challenge (no aids) and Daily Journey (route varies by date)
- No real-money purchases and no loot boxes
- Models, scenery, effects and sounds generated by code

### Technology

- Godot Engine 4.5.1
- GDScript
- GL Compatibility renderer

### O jogo

Cada mundo tem entre 3 e 7 trechos e a dificuldade aumenta de forma gradual.
Os trechos alternam entre seis atividades, sem repetir a mesma duas vezes
seguidas, nem na passagem de um mundo para o seguinte:

- **Parkour** — plataformas pequenas, ziguezagues e alturas diferentes, com salto e dash.
- **Fuga à tempestade** — uma frente vermelha avança por trás de ti. Corre, salta os obstáculos e não fiques para trás.
- **Puzzle de ecos** — grava o teu movimento num eco e ancora-o em bases azuis para materializar a ponte.
- **Escolha de percurso** — via larga e segura, ou via estreita com lasers e bónus.
- **Plataformas móveis** — plataformas que se deslocam e oscilam e que te transportam.
- **Arena de sentinelas** — drones perseguem-te na ilha. Neutraliza-os com o dash para abrir a saída.

### Características

- Viagem contínua de mundo em mundo, com dificuldade progressiva
- Bónus de domínio por trecho, que recompensam o desempenho e as escolhas
- Checkpoints, carteira de créditos e loja de skins e consumíveis
- Modos de jogo: Viagem contínua, Desafio puro (sem ajudas) e Viagem diária (percurso variado pela data)
- Sem compras com dinheiro real e sem caixas aleatórias
- Modelos, cenários, efeitos e sons gerados por código

- GDScript
- Renderizador GL Compatibility
